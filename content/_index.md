I'm a research engineer working on AI safety: **LLM evaluations, alignment,
and the reliability of agentic systems**. I'm a doctoral researcher at the
[University of Helsinki](https://www.helsinki.fi/en) (advised by
[Prof. Giulio Jacucci](https://researchportal.helsinki.fi/en/persons/giulio-jacucci))
and a founding research engineer at [SMEY.AI](https://www.smey.cc/).

In 2025 I was an AI safety research fellow at [SPAR](https://sparai.org/) and
[MARS](https://www.cambridgeaisafety.org/mars), and did the
[FAEB](https://www.tutke.org/en/finnish-alignment-engineering-bootcamp)
bootcamp, which follows the ARENA curriculum. Before that I spent 5+ years in
production ML, building training infrastructure, data pipelines, and
recommendation systems.

I'm open to research engineer roles in AI safety.
